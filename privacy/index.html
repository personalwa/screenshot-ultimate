Screenshot Ultimate Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · BestApps LLC

Screenshot Ultimate is a screenshot and annotation app for macOS made by BestApps LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

BestApps LLC does not collect or receive your screenshots, editing history, Apple Account details or payment information through Screenshot Ultimate. The app has no developer account system, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not send screenshot content to us or to Apple for purchase verification.

App Store purchases and existing access

Starting with version 1.2, unlimited Advanced editing is available through an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles payment, purchase restoration and transaction verification using StoreKit. These functions communicate with Apple's services and are subject to Apple's App Store privacy information. We do not receive your payment card or Apple Account credentials.

The app checks Apple's signed purchase records on your Mac to unlock Advanced editing and checks the version you originally downloaded so people who obtained the app before version 1.2 keep unlimited access. Those records are not uploaded to a BestApps server. Apple may provide us sales and financial reports through App Store Connect.

Local settings and free edits

App preferences and the date of your last free Advanced screenshot are stored locally on your Mac. That date enforces one free Advanced screenshot every seven days on that Mac; it is not uploaded or synced by us. Each free edit allows all changes to one screenshot until its editor window closes. Screenshot capture, Markup, saving and copying remain unlimited and free.

Your screenshots stay on your Mac

Screenshots you take, edit and save are processed entirely on your device. They are saved only where you choose, or copied to your clipboard when you ask. Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework.

Permissions

Screenshot Ultimate asks for macOS Screen Recording permission so it can capture the screen when you start a capture. It uses this permission only to take screenshots you request. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Sharing

If you use Screenshot Ultimate's Share menu, the image goes to the app or service you pick. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

Screenshot Ultimate has no advertising or tracking and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. App Store purchases are handled by Apple and can be restricted using macOS parental controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
