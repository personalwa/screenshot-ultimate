Screenshot Ultimate Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · BestApps LLC

Screenshot Ultimate is a screenshot and annotation app for macOS made by BestApps LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect any data

Screenshot Ultimate does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

Your screenshots stay on your Mac

Screenshots you take, edit and save are processed entirely on your device. They are saved only where you choose, or copied to your clipboard when you ask. Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework.

Permissions

Screenshot Ultimate asks for macOS Screen Recording permission so it can capture the screen when you start a capture. It uses this permission only to take screenshots you request. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Sharing

If you use Screenshot Ultimate's Share menu, the image goes to the app or service you pick. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

Screenshot Ultimate is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
